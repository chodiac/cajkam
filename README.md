# Čajka M: redesign "Trag" (v2, Brigade Overland art direction)

A static, fully working front end for the Čajka M tyre shop, built from the live catalogue (5,267 products) and
the shop's own Serbian copy. The earlier version (concept "Bočnica") is kept unchanged in `../cajka-m`.

```
python tools/import.py            # normalise cached raw dumps into data/*.json
python tools/import.py --fetch    # re-download products, posts and pages from cajkam.rs first
python tools/build.py             # concatenates assets/css/src/*.css and writes every page to the project root
python tools/audit.py             # every local link/asset resolves, one <h1> per page
python tools/serve.py 5188        # preview on http://localhost:5188  (launch.json: "cajka-m-v2")
python tools/live_urls.py         # refresh the list of addresses that exist on the current cajkam.rs
python tools/logos.py             # rebuild the trimmed brand logos
```

Python only, with no Node and no JS build step. The front end is plain ES modules and one generated stylesheet.
The page templates live in `tools/site_lib/` (`base.py` for layout and data, `pages_home.py`, `pages_shop.py`
and `pages_info.py`).

## Concept: "Trag" (the imprint a tyre leaves)

This takes Brigade Overland's confident editorial feel without copying it: large condensed uppercase type,
warm bone-coloured paper, dark photographic panels set inside rounded frames, and small tracked labels.
Its own design idea is **the road as the page grid**:

- **Road rail:** a dashed road marking runs down the page edge, and a small tyre rolls along it with the
  scroll position. Below 1200 px it becomes a thin yellow progress line.
- **Section labels:** every section has a small uppercase label above its heading (the original "KM 03"
  posts were removed at the client's request).
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed, which reads like road signage, is used for display and for every tyre size.
  Inter is used for text.
- **Colour:** logo slate `#243B4A`, asphalt ink and bone paper. Road-marking yellow `#F2B51C` is used only
  for actions.
- **Tread patterns** (`assets/img/tread-*.svg`, generated by the build) are the recurring graphic. They appear
  on category hovers, in the signature section, on mobile season cards and as blog placeholders.

## Hero

- **Film:** until real footage exists, `home.js` draws a canvas loop. A wheel rolls without slipping across dry
  asphalt, a wet road and snow. The weather cycles every 6.5 s, and the spray, snow powder, rain and grading
  change with it. The film pauses when it is off screen or when the button is pressed, and reduced motion
  shows one still frame.
- **Poster:** `media/hero-poster.jpg` was exported from that canvas. The dev server accepts
  `POST /__save?media/…` for this purpose only.
- **Finder:** docked across the bottom of the hero on desktop, and a stacked card under the video on phones.
  - Vozilo, Sezona, Širina / Visina R Prečnik.
  - Each option depends on the ones before it and comes from `data/web/sizes.json`, the real size index.
  - The button shows the live count ("Prikaži 22 gume").
  - Submitting goes to the real filtered catalogue URL.
- **Replacing the film:** put `media/hero.mp4` (and optionally `media/hero-720.mp4` for phones) in place and
  rebuild. The `<video>` then replaces the canvas automatically. (The on-page "Privremeni kadar" tag was removed
  at the client's request, so the temporary film is no longer labelled on the site.)
- **Phones:** the film frames the wheel above the finder (it reads the finder's position), so the shot stays
  visible on a one-screen hero.

## Signature: "Gazeći sloj"

- **Desktop:** a sticky front view of a tyre. The page scroll rolls the tread: `--roll` is the background
  offset of the tread band and of its imprint, which is shown in perspective on the road.
- **The three seasons:** as the steps scroll past, the season in the middle of the screen sets the surface,
  the tread pattern, the imprint, the particles and the temperature label:
  - Summer: dry, "Iznad 7 °C"
  - All-season: wet, "Blage zime"
  - Winter: snow, "Ispod 7 °C"
- Each step links to that season with its real in-stock count.
- **No scroll hijacking:** the steps scroll normally.
- **Phones:** three cards, each with a gently rolling tread band.
- **Reduced motion:** static.

## Motion by page

| Page | Motion |
|---|---|
| All | View transitions between pages (a tyre photo on a card morphs into the product page image). Headings rise word by word. Header hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up. Road rail. Footer "Srećan put." drifts with the scroll |
| Home | Headline and finder entrance, finder field flash when an option becomes invalid, category cards (the photo tyre turns and a tread strip rolls), film, tread section, a discount count-up, a brand belt that speeds up with scroll velocity and pauses on hover or focus, a service list that swaps images with a wipe, hotel tyres dropping onto the stack, 1992 odometer |
| Catalogue | State only: the size bar flashes on change, chips pop in, cards fade in staggered, a compare bar appears, the cart badge bumps and the added card pulses |
| Product | Photo zoom that follows the pointer (tap on phones), a sidewall-code view, the total ticking with quantity, sticky buy bar on phones |
| Service / hotel / about / blog | Image wipes, staggered reveals, sticky price-list jump bar, count-ups |

Motion is on by default for every visitor (client's request; the system "reduce motion" setting is not
used). `?motion=0` switches to the calm version for the rest of the visit (`html.calm`), and `?motion=1`
switches it back.

## Appointment form

`servis.html#zakazivanje` (also on `kontakt.html`, linked from the homepage service block): one form for
**reglaža trapa** or **zamena guma**, chosen with a toggle (`?usluga=zamena` preselects). Dates start today and
run three months ahead. Sundays are refused. Times follow the Guma Servis hours: weekdays 08:00–17:00 starts and
Saturday 08:00–15:00. The tyre fields (dimension, where the tyres are) stay in place and are only switched off
for alignment, so the form never changes height. Like the other forms, it validates and then says plainly that
nothing was sent; it needs connecting to e-mail or a booking system.

## SEO and addresses (ready for the move to cajkam.rs)

The site uses the **same addresses as the current WordPress cajkam.rs**, so the move needs almost no redirects:
`kupi-gume/<slug>/` (one static page per tyre, 5,267), `kategorija-proizvoda/<x>/` and `proizvodjac/<x>/` (with
`page/N/` pagination), `guma-servis/`, `kontakt/`, `najcesce-postavljana-pitanja/`, `YYYY/MM/DD/<post>/`, `cart/`,
`checkout/`, `my-account/`. Every product slug in the data matches its current cajkam.rs address.

Everything lives in `tools/site_lib/seo.py`:

- **`SITE_URL` / `CANONICAL_BASE` / `SWITCHED`.** While `SWITCHED = False` (preview on GitHub Pages), a page that
  already exists on cajkam.rs (`data/live-urls.txt`, refreshed with `python tools/live_urls.py`, plus every
  product) points its canonical at that cajkam.rs address, so the preview never competes with the live shop.
  Pages that are new in the redesign point at themselves on github.io. **On the move: set `SWITCHED = True`,
  rebuild, deploy.**
- **Structured data (JSON-LD) on every page:** Organization + WebSite (with site search), BreadcrumbList; TireShop +
  AutoRepair (both locations, hours, coordinates) on the home, contact, service, hotel and about pages; Product +
  Offer (RSD price, stock) on tyre pages; ItemList on category/brand pages; BlogPosting; FAQPage.
- **Head tags:** canonical, unique title and description, Open Graph/Twitter with `assets/img/og-cajkam.jpg`
  (made from the hero still by the build). `noindex` on cart, checkout, account, compare and 404.
- **Crawl files for cajkam.rs:** `sitemap.xml` (index → `sitemap-strane.xml`, `sitemap-proizvodi.xml` with
  product photos, `sitemap-clanci.xml`), `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, and `.htaccess` with 301s for the few old
  addresses that change (`REDIRECTS` in seo.py, e.g. `kako-do-nas/` → `kontakt/#kako-do-nas`). A project site on
  github.io cannot use robots.txt or a sitemap at its root, so these only take effect on cajkam.rs.
- **Old preview addresses** (`gume.html`, `blog/<slug>.html`, `guma.html?p=<slug>`) forward to the new ones.
- **Prices are a snapshot.** Static pages show price and stock at build time:
  `python tools/import.py --fetch && python tools/build.py` regularly (daily is ideal) and before the move.

Before switching off WordPress: product photos and brand logos load from `cajkam.rs/wp-content/uploads/`. Keep
that folder on the server, or move the images into the site first. After the move, verify the domain in Google
Search Console, submit `https://cajkam.rs/sitemap.xml`, and check both Google Business Profiles (shop and Guma
Servis) against the addresses, phones and hours on the site.

## Content and honesty

Everything is taken from cajkam.rs (WooCommerce Store API, WP REST API and the pages themselves). Product photos
and brand logos are hot-linked from `cajkam.rs/wp-content/uploads`. Items marked on the site with a dashed
orange tag still need to be supplied or connected:

1. **Hero footage.** Shot list: a low tracking shot of one wheel rolling from dry asphalt through a wet road
   into light snow, 8–10 s, no text or logos, 1920×1080 H.264 under 4 MB, plus a 720p cut.
2. **Photos:** the five service photos (montaža, reglaža, vulkanizer, brzi servis, perionica) and the
   warehouse/team photo for *O nama*.
3. **Missing figures:** the courier tariff, the discount for registered customers, and country of origin per
   product.
4. **Backend:** checkout, account and every form validate in the browser, then say plainly that nothing was
   sent. To go live, point the `core.js` cart at the WooCommerce Store API (`/wp-json/wc/store/v1/cart`) and
   the forms at Contact Form 7.
5. **Conflicts on the live site to resolve:**
   - Guma Servis Saturday hours: 08–16h on *kontakt* vs. 09–16h on *kako do nas*. The site uses 08–16h.
   - Payment: cash on delivery only (FAQ) vs. also by pro-forma invoice (*Uslovi korišćenja*).
   - Delivery: no deliveries at weekends vs. no deliveries on Sundays.

Load and speed indices are parsed from product names. 1,624 products have no category in the shop, so their
category is inferred from the size code. Products priced at 0 show as *Cena na upit*.
